# Hi, I'm Bishal

I'm a computational biologist at **EMBL Heidelberg**, where I hold a **Theory@EMBL Fellowship**.

Most of my work comes down to one question: how do you get a machine to read biology that wasn't written for machines? Microscopy volumes with no labels. Genomes where the signal is buried in a million features. RNA that folds into shapes nobody has annotated. I build the models that make that data legible.

I've done that across four countries in six years — India, Germany, Poland, Denmark — and the through-line has always been the same problem, just wearing different clothes.

## 🔬 What I'm working on now

**Unsupervised representation learning for 3D expansion microscopy** — EMBL Heidelberg, May 2026 → present

I'm building a modality-agnostic deep learning framework that pulls morphological features straight out of raw U-ExM image volumes. No manual segmentation, no labelled training set. Variational autoencoders and self-supervised architectures doing the work that a graduate student with a mouse used to do.

The application: ultrastructural phenotyping at scale, currently mapping centrin structural diversity across free-living unicellular eukaryotes spanning the tree of life.

`PyTorch` `VAEs` `self-supervised learning` `3D image analysis`

## 📌 Selected work

### Explainable ML for antimicrobial resistance
*Published — Bioinformatics Advances, Oxford Academic, 2025*

A resistance-prediction pipeline for *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* reaching 90%+ accuracy, with SHAP interpretability surfacing which genomic features actually drive resistance. The interpretability was the point: a black box that predicts resistance is useless to a clinician who needs to know why.

📄 [Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioadv/vbaf190) · `scikit-learn` `SHAP` `genomics`

### RNA 3D structure prediction meta-server
*ERASMUS+ research placement, IIMCB Warsaw, 2025*

An ensemble meta-server combining RosettaFoldNA and DRFold2 predictions into composite structural inputs for SimRNA. Parallelising the workflow cut end-to-end processing time by 40%.

`RosettaFoldNA` `SimRNA` `HPC`

### Structural biology × transcriptomics
*Max Planck Institute for Multidisciplinary Sciences, Göttingen, 2025–2026*

Automated AlphaFold structure-prediction pipelines evaluating the functional impact of transcriptomic change, plus analysis of 3′ UTR regulatory regions during oocyte maturation — motif discovery, RNA secondary structure, pathway enrichment. In Dr. Peter Lenart's lab.

`AlphaFold` `transcriptomics` `multi-omics`

### Castor — CRISPR guide RNA design tool
*Team project, University of Göttingen*

A full-stack tool that takes a FASTA file and returns ranked gRNA candidates. Stacking ensemble of Random Forest and XGBoost predicts efficacy from ViennaRNA-derived structural features (MFE, base-pairing probability, ensemble energy), wrapped in a Streamlit app with accounts, project management and downloadable results. I led the project and built the ML side.

💻 [Repository](https://github.com/bsa674/CRISPR-Guide-RNA-Design-Tool) · `XGBoost` `ViennaRNA` `Streamlit`

### sRNA prediction in *Salmonella enterica*
*NIT Durgapur — poster, International Conference on Global Disease Biology, 2024*

Computational prediction of small non-coding RNAs, combining ViennaRNA feature extraction with a structured ML pipeline.

`ViennaRNA` `ncRNA` `feature engineering`
